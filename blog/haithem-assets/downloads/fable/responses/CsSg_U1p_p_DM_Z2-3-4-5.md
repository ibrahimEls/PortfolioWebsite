# Degeneracy-Breaking Analysis — C
omplex Scalar Singlet + dark U(1)′ [Z2+3+4+5]

## Strategy (applies to all leaves)

The ten scan parameters split into observable handles — MDM (recoil/endpoint kinematics, γ/ν spectral endpoints), MZp and ε (visible dark-photon searches: dilepton bumps for 0.2–70 GeV and ε ≳ 10⁻³), and channel structure (light Z′ vs heavy Z′ changes the solar-neutrino spectrum hardness) — and unobservable ones (the dark quartics α2–α6, which feed only DM self-interaction at hopelessly small σ/m). My workhorse Level-1 split is the LHCb prompt dark-photon dimuon search (arXiv:1710.02867) plus B-factory visible/invisible A′ searches (arXiv:1406.2980, 1702.03327, 1808.10567): none of these are in the catalog (which recasts only the *high-mass* Z′ dilepton), and they directly cut the (MZp, ε) plane where regions differ by orders of magnitude. Sensitivity used: ε ≳ 10⁻³ prompt for 0.21 < MZp < 70 GeV (LHCb), ε ≳ 2×10⁻⁴ near 1 GeV, ε ≳ 10⁻³ below 10 GeV at B-factories. Regions whose ε range straddles the cut are assigned by their dominant/upper range; blob regions (e.g. leaf `root_no_yes` R0/R1 spanning ε 10⁻⁶–0.1) genuinely straddle and I note this honestly — the split is still the best single real measurement available. Level-2 novels: (i) solar-ν *spectrometry* at IceCube-Gen2 — annihilation to Z′ heavier than ~40 GeV gives hard ν (⟨E⟩ ~ MDM/4 ≳ 100 GeV) while GeV-scale Z′ yields µ/π that stop in the Sun before decaying, so ⟨E⟩ ≪ 100 GeV; (ii) recoil-spectrum mass fits (DARWIN-type, arXiv:1606.07001) — below the Xe target mass (~122 GeV) the spectrum shape pins m_χ to ~±15–20%, and endpoint kinematics separate 200+ GeV from ≲150 GeV; (iii) cryogenic endpoint measurements for GeV-scale DM (E_max ∝ µ²: ~75 eV at 1 GeV vs ~1.6 keV at 4.7 GeV on CaWO₄); (iv) honest speculative self-interaction probes where regions differ *only* in dark quartics (σ/m ~ λ²/(128π m_χ³) ≈ 10⁻⁷ cm²/g even at λ=10, m=5 GeV — ≥10⁵ below cluster sensitivity; flagged speculative, consistent with these being physically irreducible quartic degeneracies).

Among the latter, solar-ν hardness separates MZp ≳ 45 GeV (hard, R2,R5,R7,R10,R11,R14,R15,R22,R23,R26,R31,R41–R46) from MZp ~ 1–6 GeV (soft, rest): e.g. R2 predicts ⟨E_ν⟩ ~ 150 GeV from Z′(46–193)→qq̄, R3 (MZp 1–8, ε ≤ 10⁻⁵) predicts almost no ν above 100 GeV.
