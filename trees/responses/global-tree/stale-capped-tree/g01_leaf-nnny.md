# Degeneracy-breaking analysis — leaf `root_no_no_no_yes`

## What this leaf contains

All 176 units sit on the path {no IceCube-Gen2, BR(h→inv) < 10⁻³, CTA(WW) at 1–10× sensitivity, no DARWIN}. Three Lagrangian families are mixed:

1. **CsSg_DM.Z2+3+4+5** — pure Higgs-portal complex scalar singlet (91 units). No dark gauge sector at all; portal coupling α₁ is pinned at ~10⁻³ in every unit.
2. **CsSg_U1p[±]_DM.Z2+3+4+5** — the same singlet gauged under a dark U(1)′ (84 units), with a kinetically-mixed Z′ spanning MZp = 1 GeV–10 TeV, ε = 10⁻⁶–0.1, g′ = 0.003–12.6.
3. **CsSg_U1p[+]_DM.Z2** — one unit (R175), MDM 212–301 GeV, MZp 2.4–4.5 GeV, ε ≈ 1.5–1.9×10⁻³.

Almost every unit has MDM ≈ 97.0–97.9 GeV; the exceptions (R0: 159–247, R15: 138–183, R54: 97–180, R92–R95: 178–227, R117: 219, R175: 212–301 GeV) become a second, independent handle below.

**An honest structural point first: the U1p[+] vs U1p[−] pairs are (near-)unbreakable.** The two builds differ only in the sign of the DM's dark charge. Under dark charge conjugation combined with sᵢ → −sᵢ, q = +1 maps onto q = −1 with the phase-breaking quartics α₃, α₅ flipped in sign; leading-order spectra and rates depend on these only quadratically. No proposed measurement below (nor any I could construct) distinguishes a [+] unit from a [−] unit at matched |parameters| — I therefore spend the splitting power on the axes that *are* physical: dark-photon existence, (mA′, ε, cτ), and MDM.

## Level 1 (lit review): the low-mass dark-photon program

The catalog's only Z′ probe is the high-mass prompt pp→Z′→ℓℓ recast. The entire complementary program — **BaBar/Belle II prompt e⁺e⁻→γA′(→ℓℓ) (0.02 < mA′ < 10 GeV, ε ≳ 10⁻³ now, ~3×10⁻⁴ at 50 ab⁻¹), LHCb prompt A′→μμ (10.6–70 GeV, ε ≳ 10⁻³), and FASER2/SHiP long-lived A′ (mA′ ≲ 3–5 GeV, ε ~ 2×10⁻⁷–3×10⁻⁶)** — is a genuinely different set of measurements, and it is maximally valuable here because **a positive A′ signal excludes the pure Higgs-portal Lagrangian outright** (it predicts exactly zero in every dark-photon search), and the measured (mA′, ε, lifetime) point then pins down individual regions.

Why not the alternatives: CMB p_ann (Planck, σv ≲ 2.6×10⁻²⁵ cm³/s at 98 GeV for WW) only grazes the top of the common 1–10× CTA band and cuts across no unit boundary; dwarf-spheroidal stacking measures the same σv as the on-path CTA split (only the J-factor differs), so it cannot separate units.

## Level 2 (novel), "seen" branch: CTA Galactic-centre spectral **endpoint**

The discovery measurement itself already separates most "seen" units by (mA′, ε); what it cannot do is separate the six (mA′ ≈ 1 GeV, ε ≈ 10⁻⁶) look-alikes **R15, R54, R92, R93, R95, R117** (and R175 from its ε-neighbors). But these differ sharply in **MDM**, and the annihilation gamma-ray spectrum cuts off at E ≈ MDM: endpoint at **138–183 (R15), 97–180 (R54), 181.5 (R92), 227 (R93), 178–184 (R95), 219 (R117), 212–301 GeV (R175)** versus **97.8 ± 0.2 GeV** for R2, R16, R29, R30, R37, R56, R58, R59, R63, R66, R97, R99, R102, R107, R110. A cut "endpoint ≥ 120 GeV" separates the two classes; only R54 straddles it (assigned high; its 4 points include a ~97 GeV tail — flagged marginal). This is a new observable (spectral shape/endpoint), not a refinement of the on-path CTA *rate* split. Feasibility: CTAO GC survey, ΔE/E ≈ 7% at 100 GeV; with signals sitting at 1–10× the 500 h sensitivity, distinguishing 98 from ≥ 140 GeV endpoints needs ≲ 2× nominal exposure; dominant systematic is the GC diffuse-emission model under the endpoint. Rating: **possible**.

## Level 2 (novel), "not seen" branch: mm-vertexing short-baseline A′ spectrometer (closes the ε-gap)

The eight ε-gap units predict decay lengths squarely between "prompt" and "beam-dump": using cτ ≈ 0.3 mm × (10⁻⁴/ε)² × (GeV/mA′) — R62: 30–70 μm; R109: 0.08–0.8 mm; R7: 0.03 mm–3 cm; R100: 0.4 mm–1.5 cm; R60: 1–4 cm; R101: 1 mm–7 cm; R94: 1–19 cm; R0: μm–3 m. A DarkQuest-class 120 GeV proton fixed-target experiment upgraded with LHCb-VELO-style silicon at ~10 cm from the target, vertexing displaced e⁺e⁻/μμ from 0.1 mm to ~1 m (lab boosts γ ~ 10–50 map the cτ list onto exactly this window), covers all eight; the pure singlet and the heavy-Z′ units predict zero. Feasibility: DarkQuest already projects ε ~ 10⁻⁶–10⁻⁴ for mA′ ≲ 2 GeV with a ~5 m decay volume; the concept needs mm-scale vertex acceptance (to reach ε ~ 3×10⁻⁴) and Drell-Yan production up to mA′ ~ 10–12 GeV (for R100), i.e. a ~3–10× step in POT and background rejection against prompt Drell-Yan/charm dimuons — the dominant systematic and precisely why this gap exists. Rating: **unlikely** (dedicated next-generation effort).

**Residual degeneracy, stated plainly:** after both levels, the 146-unit "not seen / not seen" pool (pure singlet + decoupled heavy-Z′ + R57/R61/R96) remains unbroken. For heavy Z′ with small ε·g′/MZp² the U(1)′ sector decouples and the theory is *parametrically* the pure singlet plus quartic relabeling — no measurement can split it. The one future handle worth recording: the large-ε heavy-Z′ subset (R3, R43, R73, R105, R125, R129; ε up to 0.1) shifts Z-pole pseudo-observables by ~ε²mZ²/MZp² ≈ 10⁻⁴–5×10⁻⁴ for MZp ≲ 0.5 TeV, within FCC-ee/GigaZ reach — a natural third-level split if the tree format allowed one here.
