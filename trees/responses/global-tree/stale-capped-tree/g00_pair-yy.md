# Reasoning

## What these two leaves actually contain

Both leaves sit on the path {IceCube-Gen2 signal, σv ≥ 10× CTA (WW), DARWIN yes/no}, and both contain the same two *physically distinct* dark sectors:

1. **R0 in each leaf — `CsDh_DM` (complex scalar doublet)**, MDM ≈ 551–616 GeV with small quartics (α₁,α₂,α₃ ≲ 0.07). This is precisely the high-mass thermal window of an inert-doublet-like model: annihilation is *gauge-driven* (η η* → W⁺W⁻, ZZ), there is a charged partner η± nearly degenerate with the neutral state, and the neutral complex scalar is split into real states by the α₁(η H)² operator (δ ≈ α₁v²/MDM ≈ 0.1 GeV even at α₁ = 10⁻³), so tree-level Z exchange in direct detection is off and its σ_SI is Higgs-portal-like with f_n ≈ f_p.

2. **Everything else — `CsSg_U1p[±]` (complex scalar singlet + dark U(1)′)**. The DM is the lighter *real* component s_r of the dark-charged scalar; the relic is set by s s → Z′Z′ with g′ ≈ 0.3–0.5, and the Z′ decays to SM fermions through kinetic mixing ε ∈ [10⁻⁶, 0.1] with masses MZ′ ≈ 1–583 GeV. A real scalar has no diagonal Z′ current, so its σ_SI is also pure Higgs portal with f_n ≈ f_p.

Two structural points that shape everything below:

- **Direct-detection tricks fail here.** Both Lagrangians scatter through the Higgs portal with f_n ≈ f_p, so target-complementarity (Ar/Xe ratio, isospin violation) does *not* discriminate — the usually powerful "kinetic mixing couples to protons only" signature is absent because elastic Z′ exchange vanishes for the real component. Annual modulation/directionality adds nothing beyond what DARWIN's yes/no already encodes.
- **`U1p[+]` vs `U1p[-]` is a gauge redundancy.** Flipping the sign of the DM's dark charge is a charge conjugation of the dark sector: at matched (MDM, MZ′, g′, ε, αᵢ) the two builds are the *same theory*. No experiment can ever separate a [+] region from a [-] region except through their different parameter values. Similarly, `.Z2` vs `.Z2+3+4+5` builds differ only by odd dark self-couplings (s_i s_r³, s_i³ s_r), whose only observable is DM self-interaction, at these masses σ_self/m ~ α²(3/M³) ≲ 10⁻¹⁴ cm²/g — fourteen orders below cluster bounds. I therefore separate *regions*, and state honestly that build-label degeneracy at matched parameters is irreducible.

## Level 1 (both leaves): CTA Galactic-Centre monochromatic γ-line search

The catalog's CTA entries are *continuum template* fluxes (WW, bb). A monochromatic line + endpoint search is a genuinely different measurement with its own published projections (CTA GC programme, arXiv:2007.16129; line/endpoint spectroscopy for EW-multiplet DM, arXiv:2008.00692), and it cleanly separates the two **Lagrangians** — the highest-value split:

- **Doublet (R0):** the same gauge dynamics that drives the WW continuum produces loop-level γγ and γZ lines at E_γ ≈ MDM, coherently enhanced by the near-degenerate η± running in the loop (the inert-doublet line/VIB mechanism, arXiv:1306.4681). For W-annihilating EW multiplets at ~0.6 TeV the line-to-continuum ratio is ~10⁻³–10⁻². The path pins the continuum at 10–100× the CTA WW limit (≈ 6×10⁻²⁷–2×10⁻²⁶ cm³/s at 600 GeV), i.e. σv_cont ≈ 10⁻²⁵–10⁻²⁴ cm³/s, so σv(γγ+γZ/2) ≈ 10⁻²⁷–10⁻²⁶ cm³/s. CTA's line sensitivity at 0.6 TeV is ≈ (2–3)×10⁻²⁸ cm³/s (500 h GC, Einasto) with 6–8% energy resolution — a 3–50σ-equivalent margin. Crucially, because line and continuum scale with the *same* short-distance annihilation strength, sitting high above the WW limit guarantees the line is high too; the discriminator is only marginal at the extreme low end of the box.
- **Singlets (all other R):** s_r s_r → γγ requires either two powers of ε at one loop (σ_γγ ∝ ε⁴, < 10⁻³¹ cm³/s even at ε = 0.1) or the Higgs portal with α₁ ≤ 0.05 far off-shell (< 10⁻³⁰). The Z′Z′ → 4f cascade gives a smooth, box-smeared continuum with *no* feature at CTA's resolution. Prediction: no line, at least four orders of magnitude below the cut.

Cut in absolute units: **line flux corresponding to σv(γγ) ≥ 3×10⁻²⁸ cm³/s at E_γ = 0.56–0.62 TeV (leaf `root_yes_yes_yes`, MDM ∈ [560, 616]) or 0.55–0.60 TeV (leaf `root_yes_yes_no`, MDM ∈ [552, 604])**, Einasto GC J-factor. An IACT analyst needs no knowledge of this scan to test it. (Considered and rejected as Level 1: AMS-02 p̄/e⁺ spectral shape — WW vs Z′Z′ cascades do differ, but propagation and solar-modulation systematics make it a likelihood shift, not a binary split.)

This is a two-way split: `yes` → R0 alone (Lagrangian identified as the doublet); `no` → all singlet regions, which remain internally degenerate → novel node.
