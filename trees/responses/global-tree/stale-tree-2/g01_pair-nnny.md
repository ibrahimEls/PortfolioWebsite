# Degeneracy-Breaking Analysis — Leaf `root_no_no_no_yes_no`

## Why the catalog is blind here

Every unit in this leaf is, from the catalog's point of view, the *same* experiment-facing object: a ~100 GeV scalar with a Higgs-portal coupling α1 ≈ 10⁻³, annihilating to WW at 1–10× the CTA GC projection, with σ_SI below DARWIN and no ν flux. The invisible-Higgs bins are vacuous — every unit has M_DM > m_h/2, so BR(h→inv) = 0 identically. About 95% of units sit at M_DM = 97.4–97.9 GeV (the sweet spot where portal annihilation lands in the 1–10× CTA window while σ_SI evades DARWIN); a handful of U(1)′ units sit at 140–300 GeV. All SM-facing catalog rates are functions of just (M_DM, α1), which are common; the entire remaining parameter volume — the 15 dark quartics of CsSg, and (M_Z′, ε, g′) of the U(1)′ builds — is catalog-invisible. Discrimination must therefore target either (a) the Z′ sector directly (production/decay, or Z–Z′ mixing), or (b) M_DM spectroscopically.

## Level 1 (lit review): visible dark-photon search, LHCb + Belle II

The catalog's "Z′ dilepton" observable is the *prompt high-mass pp→Z′→ℓℓ* recast; the sub-70 GeV visible dark-photon program — LHCb prompt+displaced A′→μμ and Belle II e⁺e⁻→γA′(→ℓℓ) — is a genuinely different measurement absent from the catalog. Cut: an A′→ℓ⁺ℓ⁻ resonance with 0.2 < m < 70 GeV at ε ≳ 3×10⁻⁴ (the LHCb Run 3–4 / Belle II 50 ab⁻¹ projected floor).

Predicted **seen** (all U(1)′; a positive result also instantly falsifies all 91 CsSg units):
- **R30, R63** (M_Z′ = 1 GeV, ε = 0.1) and **R58** (1 GeV, ε = 0.004–0.014), **R99** (1 GeV, ε = 0.0027–0.0041): 3–100× above the *current* BaBar bound (ε ≲ 10⁻³ at 1 GeV) — already excluded by data the RL scan did not impose; any rerun of these searches "sees" them trivially.
- **R102** (17.6–36.6 GeV, ε = 0.1) and **R110** (3.4–7.9 GeV, ε ≈ 0.08–0.1): grossly visible in LHCb dimuon (σ·BR scales as ε² ~ 10⁻²; current limits there are ε ~ 10⁻³).
- **R175** (2.4–4.5 GeV, ε = 1.5–1.9×10⁻³): at/above current BaBar; Belle II definitive. Notably the *only Z2-only build* in the leaf, so this split also tags the discrete-symmetry variant.
- **R56** (2.4–5.5 GeV, ε = 3.2–8.3×10⁻⁴), **R92** (1 GeV, 4.3×10⁻⁴), **R29** (1 GeV, 2.1–6.6×10⁻⁴): inside the ≈(2–3)×10⁻⁴ projected reach; R29's lower edge is marginal (flagged honestly).

**Not seen**: everything else — all CsSg units (no Z′ exists), all TeV-scale-Z′ units (out of mass range), and light-Z′ units with ε ≲ 3×10⁻⁴ (R62 at 2.1–3.0×10⁻⁴ is just below the cut; marginal). Per the one-message constraint I could not re-resolve the arXiv IDs with tools this turn; I cite only three IDs I am confident of: LHCb A′→μμ (1910.06926), the Belle II Physics Book (1808.10567), and the dark-photon review (2005.01515).

## Level 2 (novel), attached to the big "not seen" outcome — three concepts, applied in sequence

**Novel 1 — CTA GC spectral-endpoint fit (rating: possible).** This is *not* a refinement of the path's "see anything at CTA(WW)?" detection threshold: given the leaf premise that a signal *is* seen at 1–10× the 500 h limit, the new observable is the *energy* of the spectral cutoff, E_cut ≈ M_DM, in GeV. Predictions: **yes** (E_cut ≥ 130 GeV) for R93 (227 GeV), R94 (195–215), R117 (219), R0 (159–247), R95 (178–184), R15 (138–183), and R54 (97–180 — straddles the cut, flagged marginal); **no** (E_cut = 97.0–97.9 GeV) for the other 159 units. CTA energy resolution ≈7% at 100 GeV separates 98 from ≥140 GeV at ≫3σ for any detected flux; dominant systematic is GC diffuse-emission modeling biasing the fitted cutoff. Empirically all seven "yes" units are U(1)′ builds, so this is Lagrangian-separating too. Rating **possible**: existing/funded instrument, no sensitivity improvement needed — only spectroscopy on an already-assumed signal.

**Novel 2 — near-dump displaced-vertex Z′ spectrometer (rating: unlikely).** Closest instrument: DarkQuest (SpinQuest spectrometer, 120 GeV FNAL Main Injector, tracking 5–12 m from the dump, ~10¹⁸ POT proposed), whose projections already touch ε ~ 10⁻⁶–10⁻⁴ for 0.2–2 GeV. Proposed upgrade: instrument the decay region from ~1 m (decay length γcτ ≈ 2.4 m·(10⁻⁶/ε)²·(1 GeV/m_Z′) at γ≈30, i.e. cm-to-10 m across the target band), ~10²⁰ POT, ECAL for e⁺e⁻, MeV-level vertex mass resolution; masses 0.3–8 GeV via bremsstrahlung + Drell-Yan. Predicted **seen**: the 21 light-Z′, small-ε units — comfortably R7, R60, R62, R94, R100, R101, R109 (ε = 10⁻⁵–3×10⁻⁴); intermediate R2, R16, R59, R61 (few×10⁻⁶); at the coverage edge R0, R15, R37, R54, R66, R93, R95, R97, R107, R117 (ε at the 10⁻⁶ scan floor — flagged). Dominant systematic: beam-related neutron and combinatorial muon background at short decay lengths. Improvement over DarkQuest ≈10²× POT plus near vertexing → **unlikely**. Honest failures left on the "no" side: R96 (M_Z′ = 19 GeV) and R57 (47–49 GeV) with ε ≤ 2×10⁻⁵ — above dump kinematics (√s ≈ 15 GeV) and hopeless at colliders (rates ∝ ε² ~ 10⁻¹⁰).

**Novel 3 — Tera-Z electroweak Z–Z′ mixing fit (rating: unlikely).** Kinetic mixing with a *massive* Z′ induces mass mixing θ ≈ ε s_W M_Z²/(M_Z′² − M_Z²), shifting sin²θ_eff and the Z lineshape. Predictions: R3 (ε = 0.03–0.1, M_Z′ = 66–1459 GeV, near-resonant portion) θ up to O(10⁻²) — partly LEP-dead already; R129 (375–433 GeV, ε = 0.045–0.1) θ ≈ 2–7×10⁻³; R98 (115–209 GeV, ε = 0.7–1.9×10⁻³, near-resonant enhancement) θ ≈ 2–6×10⁻⁴; R43 and R125 (1–4 TeV, ε = 0.05–0.1) θ ≈ 0.5–4×10⁻⁴ (marginal, flagged). Every other unit predicts θ < 10⁻⁴ (ε ≤ 10⁻³ or M_Z′ ≥ 3 TeV) — or θ = 0 exactly for CsSg. An FCC-ee-class Tera-Z run (δsin²θ_eff ~ 5×10⁻⁶, ~20× beyond LEP; dominant systematic: beam-energy calibration by resonant depolarization) reaches θ ~ 10⁻⁴ → cut |Δsin²θ_eff| ≥ 10⁻⁵. Needs a dedicated next-generation machine within its published projections → **unlikely**.

**Honest residual.** After all three nodes, the remaining degenerate mass is (i) the 91 CsSg units vs U(1)′ units with TeV-scale Z′ and ε ≲ 10⁻³ (R13, R17, R24, R31, R32, R38–R40, …): the only lever there is an improved high-mass dilepton search, which is the catalog observable and is anyway dead at σ ∝ ε² for ε ≤ 10⁻²; and (ii) the *internal* CsSg region distinctions, which differ only in dark quartics α2–α16 — these change no SM-facing rate, and the induced DM self-interaction is σ/m ~ 10⁻¹⁰ cm²/g even at λ = 10, twelve orders below cluster bounds. These degeneracies are genuinely unresolvable by any realistic measurement and are left on the terminal "no" branches rather than papered over. (Side note for the record: units with g′ = 7–12.6, e.g. R18, R41, R72, R122, R126, R128, have α_dark ≳ 4 — nonperturbative inputs whose viability is a theory-consistency question, not a measurement.)
