2. **Dark-U(1)′ scalars with an accessible Z′** — regions whose kinetic mixing ε and Z′ mass land inside current/planned dark-photon search coverage (MZ′ ≲ 70 GeV, ε ≳ 3×10⁻⁴).
3. **Dark-U(1)′ scalars with a decoupled Z′** — MZ′ up to 10 TeV, or ε down to 10⁻⁶, invisible to any dilepton/dark-photon program.

The catalog's Z′-dilepton observable is a high-mass prompt Drell–Yan σ×BR recast; it does **not** cover the low-mass (0.2–70 GeV), ε-driven dark-photon regime probed by radiative-return and inclusive prompt-dimuon searches. That regime is exactly where a large fraction of the U(1)′ regions live — so this is a genuinely new measurement, not a refinement of anything on the path.

**Level 1 — LHCb prompt A′→μμ + Belle II e⁺e⁻→γA′(→ℓℓ) visible dark-photon search.**
