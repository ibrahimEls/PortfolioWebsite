## Why this leaf is invisible to the catalog, and what the catalog missed

Every unit in this leaf is the same field content — a complex scalar singlet `s = (sr + i·si)/√2` with dark charge −1 under a gauged U(1)′ with gauge boson Z′ — in two symmetry variants (Z2: quartics α1–α4; Z2+3+4+5: adds the odd cross-quartics α3·si·sr³ and α5·si³·sr). Three structural facts govern everything:

1. **The Z′ is always a *visible* dark photon.** MZp ∈ [1, 140] GeV while MDM ∈ [105, 316] GeV, so MZp < 2·MDM in every one of the 140 units: the decay Z′→DM DM is kinematically closed and the Z′ decays 100% back to SM fermions through its kinetic mixing ε with the photon. ε spans **five decades (10⁻⁶ to 0.1)** across the units — and *no observable in our 18-item catalog is a low-mass dilepton bump hunt* (the catalog's "Z′ dilepton" entry is the HL-LHC high-mass Drell-Yan σ×BR recast, which starts above ~150–200 GeV; every MZp here is below 140 GeV, and mostly below 70 GeV).

2. **Direct detection is switched off by inelasticity, not small couplings.** The Z′ coupling to a complex scalar is purely off-diagonal in the (sr, si) basis: g′Z′μ(sr ∂μ si − si ∂μ sr). The portal term α1·H²·sr² gives mass only to sr after electroweak symmetry breaking, splitting sr from si by δm ≈ α1v²/(2·MDM) ≈ 30–300 MeV for α1 ~ 10⁻³–4×10⁻³. A splitting of tens of MeV is ~10⁵ times the ~100 keV kinematic ceiling for inelastic scattering, so the potentially enormous Z′-exchange nuclear cross-section (which for ε = 0.1, MZp = 1 GeV would naively be ~10⁻³³ cm²) is completely forbidden, and only the tiny α1 elastic portal survives — consistent with "nothing at XLZD" even at ε = 0.1. This is why the catalog's entire DD column is blind here.

3. **The relic is secluded:** gU1p ∈ [0.12, 0.32] rises tightly with MDM ∈ [105, 316] GeV across all 140 units — the classic secluded-WIMP band where χχ* → Z′Z′ (s-wave, always open) sets Ωh², so ⟨σv⟩₀ ≈ 2×10⁻²⁶ cm³/s in *every* unit, hiding just under the catalog's CTA(WW)/Fermi(WW) template reach because the true spectrum is a Z′Z′→4-fermion cascade, not WW.

So the discriminating axes the catalog cannot see are exactly: **(ε, MZp)** via dark-photon bump hunts, and **(MZp hadronic vs leptonic, MDM)** via the secluded-cascade annihilation products.
